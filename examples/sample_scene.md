# Sample Scene

Lin Qiao entered the archive after sunset. The room smelled of damp rope and old glue. Director Han looked up from the table first, then the two clerks looked at her as if she had brought the weather in with her. Her wet hair clung to her face, and for a moment the room seemed to measure her by the shape she made in the doorway.

She put the storm chart on the table. Nobody touched it.

"You restored the missing reef," Director Han said.

"I restored what was under the ink," Lin Qiao said.

The youngest clerk smiled as if the answer was charming. Lin Qiao kept her hand on the map case and felt the warped brass latch press a line into her palm. Outside, rainwater ran through the stone gutters toward the empty wells.
