# Sample Character Seed

## Name

Lin Qiao

## Role

Map restorer and reluctant witness.

## Desire

She wants the restored maps to tell the truth, but she also wants her family to survive the town's anger.

## Contradiction

She trusts paper more than people, yet every map she repairs forces her into public conflict.

## Private Pressure

Her grandmother once worked in the rain wells and left behind a notebook with pages removed.

## Strength

Patient attention, practical courage, and the ability to read damage as evidence.

## Blind Spot

She mistakes silence for safety.
