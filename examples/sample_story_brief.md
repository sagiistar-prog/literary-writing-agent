# Sample Story Brief

## Working Title

The Rain Collector

## Premise

In a coastal town where the old rain wells have dried, a young map restorer named Lin Qiao discovers that every repaired map changes one small thing in the town by morning. When the harbor committee orders her to restore a century-old storm chart, she must decide whether to preserve the town's official history or reveal the erased route that once saved a group of women who refused to leave during a typhoon.

## Emotional Core

The story is about memory, inheritance, and the cost of being believed too late.

## Desired Texture

Quiet suspense, salt air, paper fibers, civic rituals, weather records, family silence, and the unease of a town that edits its own past.

## Constraints

- Original fiction only.
- No imitation of living authors.
- No protected source text.
- Emphasize female agency and community memory.
